# Data Audit

Based on raw audit generated by `python src/audit_data.py`.\
Files audited:
`player_game_logs.csv` (424,478 rows), `team_game_logs.csv` (33,316 rows).\
Coverage: 2010-11 to 2023-24, regular season only.

## Summary

The data is clean. There are no duplicates, no impossible box-score values, and player stats reconcile exactly with team totals. There were some small issues such as inconsistent team abbreviations.

## 3. Duplicates

- Fully duplicated rows: 0
- Duplicate `(player_id, game_id)`: 0
- Same player on two teams in the same game or on the same date: 0
- Duplicate `(game_id, team_id)` in team logs: 0

## 4. Weird rows

**Usage over 60%: 392 rows.** 388 of 392 are under 10 minutes, with a median of 1.8 minutes (max usage 160.7%, Jack Cooley, 1.1 min). The formula blows up when a player plays about a minute and takes a shot or two, so these rows make sense.
The 4 rows with 10+ minutes are plausible high-volume games (Kobe's 60-point finale, a 57-point Harden game, Will Bynum 15 FGA in 12 min, Anthony Davis 18 FGA in 14 min).

**Traded players:** Ranges from 39 to 105 players per season appear on 2+ teams, and all of them have a different `team_id` on different dates, so nobody is on two teams in the same game or on the same day.

**Other checks:**
- 2 team IDs have 2 abbreviations each (franchise renames).

## 5. Cross-checks

- Sum of player `pts` and `fga` equals team totals in all 33,316 team-games.
- Every game has exactly 2 teams; `opp_team_id` always points to the other team.
- Team pace, ortg, and drtg are in plausible ranges (pace 79-121, ortg/drtg 60-154).


## 6. Changes made

Normalized the two renamed team abbreviations to the current ones: NOH→NOP, NJN→BKN
